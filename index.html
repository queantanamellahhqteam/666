Freemasonry ni nini? What is Freemasonry?

Freemasonry ni udugu wa kimataifa wa wanaume unaofundisha maadili, urafiki na kuisaidia jamii. Asili yake ni vyama vya mafundi wa mawe wa Ulaya ya zama za kati, na Grand Lodge ya kwanza ilianzishwa London mwaka 1717. Freemasonry is an international fraternity that teaches morals, friendship and service to the community. It grew out of the guilds of stonemasons in medieval Europe, and the first Grand Lodge was founded in London in 1717.

Wanakutana wapi?Where do they meet?
Kwenye “lodge”, yaani kikundi cha eneo fulani kinachokutana mara kwa mara.In a “lodge”, a local group that meets regularly.
Wanafanya nini?What do they do?
Hufanya ibada za kiishara zinazotumia zana za mafundi wa mawe, kama kipimo cha pembe na bikari, kufundisha maadili. Pia huchangia kazi za hisani.They perform symbolic ceremonies that use stonemasons’ tools, like the square and compasses, to teach moral lessons. They also raise money for charity.
Je, ni dini?Is it a religion?
Hapana. Haichukui nafasi ya imani yako, ila mwanachama anatakiwa kuamini Kiumbe Mkuu. Siasa na mabishano ya dini hayaruhusiwi kwenye mikutano yao.No. It does not replace your faith, but members are expected to believe in a Supreme Being. Politics and religious arguments are not allowed at lodge meetings.
Je, ni siri?Is it secret?
Uwepo wao si siri. Kinachobaki faragha ni mila na ishara za ndani za ibada zao.Its existence is not. What stays private are the inner customs and signs of its ceremonies.
Alama yaoThe symbol
Kipimo cha pembe (Square) na bikari (Compasses), mara nyingi na herufi G katikati. G inasimama kwa “God” (Mungu) au “Geometry” (jiometri).The Square and Compasses, often with a G in the middle. The G stands for “God” or “Geometry”.
DigriiDegrees
Mwanachama hupanda ngazi tatu: Entered Apprentice, Fellow Craft na Master Mason.Members progress through three degrees: Entered Apprentice, Fellow Craft and Master Mason.

Je, Freemasonry inatoa utajiri? Does Freemasonry give you wealth?

Hapana.No.

Freemasonry haitoi pesa, kazi, mikopo wala cheo kwa wanachama wake, na hakuna ushahidi kwamba imewahi kufanya hivyo. Freemasonry does not give members money, jobs, loans or rank, and there is no evidence that it ever has.

  • Wanachama ndio wanaolipa: ada ya kuingia, ada ya kila mwaka na michango ya hisani.Members are the ones who pay: an initiation fee, yearly dues and charity donations.
  • Mafundisho yao yanamtaka mgombea ajiunge bila kutegemea faida ya kifedha.Its teachings expect a candidate to join without hoping for financial gain.
  • Mtu anaweza kupata marafiki na marafiki wa kikazi, lakini hiyo si ahadi ya utajiri.Members can make friends and useful contacts, but that is not a promise of riches.

Jihadhari na utapeliBeware of scams

Baadhi ya matapeli hutumia jina la Freemasonry kuiba pesa. Ishara za kuangalia:Some scammers use the name Freemasonry to steal money. Warning signs:

  • Ahadi ya utajiri wa haraka.A promise of fast wealth.
  • Kuombwa pesa za simu au benki ili “uunganishwe” na Freemasons.Being asked to send mobile money or bank transfers to be “connected” to the Freemasons.
  • Kuambiwa ufanye kafara au tambiko ili upate mali.Being told to perform a sacrifice or ritual to get rich.

Usitume pesa kwa mtu yeyote anayeahidi njia ya mkato ya kuingia.Never send money to anyone who promises a shortcut into the society.

Sheria zake Its rules

Kila Grand Lodge ina katiba yake, kwa hiyo sheria hutofautiana kidogo kati ya nchi na nchi. Hizi ndizo kanuni zinazojulikana zaidi: Every Grand Lodge has its own constitution, so rules differ a little from country to country. These are the best-known principles:

  • Kuamini Kiumbe Mkuu, kwa dini yoyote.Belief in a Supreme Being, of any religion.
  • Kuwa mtu mzima mwenye tabia njema anayejiunga kwa hiari yake mwenyewe.Being an adult of good character who joins of your own free will.
  • Kutii sheria za nchi.Obeying the laws of your country.
  • Kutojadili siasa wala kubishana kuhusu dini kwenye mikutano ya lodge.No politics and no religious arguments in lodge meetings.
  • Kutunza siri za ndani za ibada. Hizi ni mila na ishara, si taarifa za kuficha uhalifu.Keeping the inner customs of the ceremonies private. These are customs and signs, not information for hiding crimes.
  • Kuishi kwa upendo wa kidugu, kusaidia wenye shida na kusema ukweli.Living by brotherly love, relief for those in need, and truth.
  • Kwa mujibu wa mafundisho yao, wajibu kwa Mungu, nchi na familia hutangulia.By their own teaching, duty to God, country and family comes first.

Freemasonry ya kawaida ni ya wanaume. Zipo lodge chache za wanawake na za pamoja, zinazoendeshwa na mashirika tofauti. Regular Freemasonry is for men. A few lodges for women, and mixed lodges, exist under separate organisations.

Jinsi ya kujiunga How to join

Kwa kawaida Freemasons hawatafuti wanachama kwa kuwashawishi watu. Mtu huomba mwenyewe, kwa hiari yake. Freemasons usually do not recruit by persuading people. A person asks to join of their own free will.

  1. Tafuta lodge halali.Find a genuine lodge. Tafuta Grand Lodge ya nchi yako kupitia tovuti yao rasmi. Njia moja ya kuthibitisha ni kuangalia kama inatambuliwa na United Grand Lodge of England. Look for the Grand Lodge of your country through its official website. One way to check is whether it is recognised by the United Grand Lodge of England.
  2. Hakikisha unakidhi mahitaji ya msingi.Check the basic requirements. Mwanamume, mwenye umri wa chini unaotakiwa (mara nyingi miaka 18 hadi 21), anayeamini Kiumbe Mkuu na mwenye tabia njema. A man above the minimum age (often 18 to 21) who believes in a Supreme Being and has good character.
  3. Wasiliana nao.Get in touch. Wasiliana na lodge, au mwombe mwanachama unayemfahamu akupendekeze. Unaweza kualikwa kwenye mazungumzo au tukio la kijamii kwanza. Contact the lodge, or ask a member you know to propose you. You may be invited to a conversation or a social event first.
  4. Subiri uchunguzi na kura.Wait for the review and vote. Wanachama huangalia wasifu wako, kisha hupiga kura ya kukukubali au kutokukubali. Members look into your background, then vote on whether to accept you.
  5. Sherehe ya kuingizwa.The initiation. Ukikubaliwa, unakaribishwa rasmi kama mwanachama wa digrii ya kwanza. If accepted, you are formally welcomed as a member of the first degree.

Gharama na uhuruCost and freedom

Ada hutofautiana kwa kila lodge. Lipa kupitia lodge yenyewe pekee, kamwe si kwa mtu binafsi anayeahidi njia ya mkato. Mwanachama anaweza kujiuzulu wakati wowote. Fees vary by lodge. Pay only through the lodge itself, never to an individual offering a shortcut. A member can resign at any time.
